# Modern Kallaa Petti: Privacy Policy

Effective from 4 October 2026.

Modern Kallaa Petti ("the app") is a free billing, stock and accounts app for
Android, written by Dr Aran Castro. This policy explains what the app does with
your information. In short: your business data stays on your phone, and the app
has no account, no server, no advertising and no tracking.

## 2. What the app stores, and where

The app stores the following on your phone only, in its private storage:

- your business profile (name, address, phone, email, GSTIN, UPI ID, bank details,
  Udyam number, logo, signature);
- your customers and suppliers (name, phone, GSTIN, address, balances);
- your items, bills, estimates, returns, payments and expenses;
- your settings (theme, bill numbering, printer, app lock on or off).

None of this is sent to the developer or to any server run by the developer. The
developer cannot see it.

## 3. What leaves your phone, and only when you choose

- **Sharing a bill or report.** When you tap WhatsApp, Share PDF, Print or Share
  as Excel, the file goes to the app or printer you pick. What that app does with
  it is governed by its own policy (for example WhatsApp's).
- **Backup files.** "Back up now" saves a file where you choose (Google Drive,
  Downloads, a pen drive). The file holds all your data and is not password
  protected. Keep it somewhere private. The app also keeps up to seven automatic
  copies inside its own storage; they are deleted when you uninstall the app.
- **Android Auto Backup.** Android may keep an encrypted copy of the app's data
  in your Google account when your phone has a screen lock. You can switch this
  off in your phone's backup settings.
- **Bluetooth printing.** Receipts are sent to the Bluetooth printer you select.

## 4. Permissions

| Permission | Why |
|---|---|
| Nearby devices (Bluetooth) | To print receipts on your paired thermal printer. Used only when you print or choose a printer. |
| Internet | Declared because the Google barcode scanner module is downloaded by Google Play services the first time you scan. The app itself does not send your business data over the internet. |
| Contacts | The app does **not** hold the contacts permission. When you choose "From contacts", Android's own contact picker opens; only the name and phone number of the contact you pick are given to the app. |

The app does not use your location, camera permission, microphone, photos library
or SMS. Choosing a logo or signature uses the Android photo picker, which gives
the app only the image you select.

## 5. Barcode scanning

Barcode scanning uses Google's code scanner (ML Kit through Google Play
services). Scanning happens on your phone. Google Play services may collect
technical and usage information as described in Google's own terms and privacy
policy; that information is handled by Google, not by this app.

## 6. UPI payment codes

The UPI QR code on a bill or at the counter is created on your phone from your
UPI ID and the amount. The app does not process, see or store any payment. The
payment is made between the customer's UPI app and your bank.

## 7. App lock

If you switch on App lock, the app asks for your fingerprint, face or phone
screen lock using Android's own system. The app never sees your fingerprint or
PIN. While the lock is on, screenshots and the recent-apps picture of the app
are blocked.

## 8. Children

The app is meant for business owners and is not directed at children.

## 9. Your choices and deleting your data

- To delete the data in the app, use the phone's Settings › Apps › Modern
  Kallaa Petti › Storage › Clear data, or uninstall the app.
- Backup files you saved yourself (for example on Google Drive) must be deleted
  by you.
- Customers' details belong to your business. You are responsible for using them
  in line with the law that applies to your business.
